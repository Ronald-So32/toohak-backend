1. I have last used kahoot sometime during term two of 2024.
2. Other formats that I am keen to see Kahoot innovate into their systems with would be to integrate sending images as a function to answer questions.
3. In many instances, I believe that images speak louder than words which can make for a more immersive experience for users, making them more engaged and focused in answering questions.
4. In my experience with an online queuing system, I have found that players are much more competitive and may cause other emotions to swell up  such as anger, hate and online harassment. These incidents have high levels of online abuse which may intimidate new users into using an alternative app.
5. I would love to see a ranked system being added into Kahoot/any other quiz app as this may make it far more entertaining and engaging for users.
6. I do not believe it is appropriate to add emotes into quiz-like apps as this distracts users from the purpose of the quiz and can be used to enrage/mentally hurt other participants from answering the quiz.
7. Sure, the voting/queuing system can give more time for participants to mentally prepare themselves and perhaps make them more relaxed going into the quiz.
